\section{Simulating a general r.v. using a uniform r.v.}

\hyperref[thm-rvexistence]{Theorem~\ref*{thm-rvexistence}} from Section
\ref{sec:dist-funs}, which we proved earlier for its
theoretical importance, can be interpreted about a
statement about how to simulate a random variable with
a specified distribution $F$ using a $U[0,1]$ random
variable.

$\textbf{Simulation Method 4.}$ Given a c.d.f. $F$
and a $U[0,1]$-distributed random variable $U$, set
$X=g(U)$, where

\[
g(p) = \sup \{ x\in\R\,:\, F(x) < p \} \qquad (0<p<1).
\]

\noindent (this is the lower quantile function associated with
$F$; see Section \ref{sec:dist-funs}).

Explain why the above method works and its connection
to \hyperref[thm-rvexistence]{Theorem~\ref*{thm-rvexistence}}.
\end{xexercise}
