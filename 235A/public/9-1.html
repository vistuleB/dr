9.1 Weak laws of large numbers

Theorem 9.1

Weak Law of Large Numbers (WLLN)If $\mathbf{E}|X_1|<\infty$ then

$$ \frac{S_n}{n} \xrightarrow[n\to\infty]{\mathrm{P}} \mu. $$

Proof in the case of finite varianceIn the most general case, proving this requires some work -- we shall deduce it from its stronger cousin, the Strong Law of Large Numbers. However, if we assume that $\sigma^2 = \mathbf{V}(X_1)<\infty$, the proof is extremely easy! It suffices to note that in this case $\mathbf{V}(S_n) = n \sigma^2$ (this is true even when the $X_n$'s are not independent but only uncorrelated), or $\mathbf{V}(S_n/n) = \sigma^2/n$. From Chebyshev's inequality we then have that for any $\epsilon>0$,

$$ \begin{eqnarray*} \tag{7} \mathrm{P}(|n^{-1} S_n - \mu|>\epsilon) \le \frac{\sigma^2}{n \epsilon^2} \xrightarrow[n\to\infty]{} 0. \end{eqnarray*} $$

A$\square$
