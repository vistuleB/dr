8.1 The Bernoulli distribution

The Bernoulli distribution models the probabilistic experiment of a single coin toss. We say that $X$ has the Bernoulli distribution with parameter $0<p<1$, and denote $X \sim \operatorname{Ber}(p)$, if $X$ satisfies

$$ \mathrm{P}(X=0)=p = 1-\mathrm{P}(X=1). $$

$\textbf{Properties:}$

  1. $\mathbf{E} X = p$, $\mathbf{V}(X) = p(1-p)$.

  2. $\operatorname{Ber}(1/2)$ is the distribution that maximizes the variance $\mathbf{V}(X)$ subject to the constraint that $0\le X\le 1$.
