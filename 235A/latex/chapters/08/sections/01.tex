\section{The Bernoulli distribution}

The Bernoulli distribution models the probabilistic
experiment of a single coin toss. We say that $X$ has
the Bernoulli distribution with parameter $0<p<1$,
and denote $X \sim \operatorname{Ber}(p)$, if $X$
satisfies

\[
\mathrm{P}(X=0)=p = 1-\mathrm{P}(X=1).
\]

\noindent $\textbf{Properties:}$

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item $\mathbf{E} X = p$, $\mathbf{V}(X) = p(1-p)$.

\item $\operatorname{Ber}(1/2)$ is the distribution that
maximizes the variance $\mathbf{V}(X)$ subject to the
constraint that $0\le X\le 1$.
\end{enumerate}
